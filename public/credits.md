## A320 engine startup

**“Airbus A320 United Airlines Engines Startup” — Alejpix**

- Source: https://freesound.org/people/Alejpix/sounds/784355/
- License: Creative Commons Attribution–NonCommercial 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/
- Files: `audio/engine-1.mp3`, `audio/engine-2.mp3`, `audio/turbofan.mp3`.
- Changes: 24-second startup excerpts from 00:15 and 01:04, plus a 16-second turbine-loop excerpt from 01:36 of the public MP3 preview; mono downmix, spectral noise reduction, high/low-pass filtering, midrange attenuation, gentle expansion, loudness normalization, fade-in/out, MP3 re-encoding. Original startup pitch preserved. The turbine loop is crossfaded at runtime, then gently varied in pitch with throttle; it is not a recorded takeoff.
- This is an A320 cabin recording from a United flight, not an airline-specific exterior recording. Cabin noise is reduced, not completely isolated. Both excerpts serve a shortened simulator startup, not an exact engine procedure.
- **The recording and its adaptations are for noncommercial use only.** This condition applies even though other project assets have different licenses.

Gear hydraulics and the latch are synthesized by project code and synchronized with actuator motion. They are not real A320 recordings.

## Original code and generated content

Terrain, runway layouts, water and sky shaders, clouds, runway lighting, UI, wind, a quiet supplemental turbine layer, switches, and brake rumble are generated by project code. The fallback mechanical effect is synthesized. There is no claim that the generated sounds are real A320 recordings.

Audio preparation is reproducible with `npm run audio:prepare`. It uses FFmpeg (https://ffmpeg.org/) through the development-only `ffmpeg-static` package. FFmpeg is not shipped to the browser.
